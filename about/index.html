Small Wise One: A Journey of Joy and Whimsy

Hand-drawn illustration, clever paisley doodles, and art that turns everyday moments into something extraordinary.

Origin

The name “Small Wise One” was inspired by a baby book my mom had while picking names for me. “Loretta” means “Small Wise One,” perfectly encapsulating my journey. From a young age, I’ve been captivated by the world of art and design, always drawing and creating with insatiable curiosity.

Mission

Art is my sanctuary, a place where my busy mind finds tranquility, and my hands create beauty. Through “Small Wise One,” I aim to bring that same sense of joy and whimsy to people’s lives. I believe that art can transform everyday moments into something extraordinary.

Values

My background in interior design and efficient space planning has taught me the importance of thoughtful and functional design. I am driven by a deep appreciation for nature’s beauty and the small, often unnoticed details that hold profound design and elegance.

Unique Selling Proposition

“Small Wise One” is an illustration business offering prints and products featuring my unique drawings. My designs are known for their cleverness, whimsy, and the ability to evoke a sense of fun and delight. Each piece is crafted with a keen eye for detail and a love for the natural world.

Vision

While my focus is currently on illustrations, prints, and products, I envision expanding “Small Wise One” to include interior design in the future. I also aspire to license my designs to companies, bringing my art to a broader audience. Above all, creating art is essential for my mental well-being and allows me to spend quality time with my family and travel.

Impact

I want people to see my products and think, “That is so fun! I would love to use or gift that!” By sharing my unique design style and observations, I hope to inspire others to find joy in the small details and appreciate the beauty around them.
